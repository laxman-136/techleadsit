Cookie Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This page explains cookies and browser storage used with the Oracle Fusion Financials enquiry page. The broader website has a company Cookie Policy.

Storage used by this landing page

ItemPurpose and duration
financials-attribution
Session storage		Stores campaign information, referring and landing-page addresses, and identifiers during the browser tab’s session. It is updated when a new campaign is detected. Browsers normally clear session storage when the tab is closed, although session restoration can preserve it.
_ga, _fbp, _fbc
Analytics and advertising cookies		Google Analytics and Meta tags may set these identifiers, and the enquiry script reads them when accessible for attribution. Their lifetime is controlled by the service and its configuration.
Campaign URL parametersUTM values and advertising click IDs identify the campaign associated with an enquiry. They are not cookies, but can be stored with the enquiry.

Analytics and advertising

The published landing and policy pages load our Google Tag Manager container. It manages Google Analytics, Meta Pixel and other configured advertising tags to measure page visits and completed enquiries. A lead-completion event is sent only after mobile verification and successful registration. These services may receive browser, device, page and campaign information and set or read cookies under the site’s tracking configuration.

Your choices

You can block or clear cookies and site storage through your browser settings. This may affect site features or attribution. This page does not include a cookie-preference manager. Contact consent in the enquiry form is for demo and course follow-up; it is not consent to personalised advertising. Where optional tracking requires consent, that consent must be obtained before the tracking is activated.

Questions

Contact info@techleadsit.com about tracking or personal-data requests. Our Privacy Policy explains how enquiry information is handled.
