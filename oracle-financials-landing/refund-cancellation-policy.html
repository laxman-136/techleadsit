Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The free demo requires no payment. For paid enrollments, the following summarises Tech Leads IT’s published Refund Policy.

Refund requests

Email info@techleadsit.com within seven days of enrollment. Include the course, enrollment date and reason. Discounted purchases, special offers and completed courses are excluded under the published policy.

Review and payment

The stated review period is five business days. Approved refunds are processed within ten business days through the original payment method. Duplicate-payment refunds are stated to be processed within five business days.

Cancellation

The website separately allows cancellation within the first three classes after the demo. Cancellation does not automatically establish refund eligibility. Ask the team how the class-based cancellation window and seven-day refund window apply to your enrollment before paying.

Access problems and batch changes

Report technical access problems promptly. For a batch transfer, fee clarification or cancellation request, contact support. This page does not add a transfer entitlement or change the published exclusions.
