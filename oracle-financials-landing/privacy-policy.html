Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This notice covers the Oracle Fusion Financials enquiry and demo pages operated by Tech Leads IT Solutions Private Limited. It supplements our company Privacy Policy.

Information collected through this form

  • Your name, email address, mobile number and selected career background, learning need, intended start date, willingness to consider paid training and enrollment decision-maker.
  • Your contact-consent choice, verification status, request reference and form progress. After you give contact consent, entered details can be saved before final submission, including if verification is incomplete.
  • The referring website, landing-page address, campaign parameters such as UTM source and campaign, advertising click identifiers, and a session identifier. Existing Google Analytics or Meta identifiers may accompany the request when available.

Why we use it

We use these details to verify your mobile number by SMS, respond to your enquiry, arrange a demo, deliver a requested curriculum, discuss the relevant course, resolve registration problems and understand which campaigns generate enquiries. The forms request permission to contact you about the demo or curriculum you request and the related course; it does not enroll you in unrelated promotional communications.

Lead handling and service providers

Enquiries are stored in our Conversion Bridge lead-management system. Our team can send them to our CRM for follow-up. Where enabled, Microsoft Teams notifications alert the admissions team. SMS delivery, hosting and relevant service providers process information needed for their role. Our company policy states that personal information is not sold, rented or traded.

Retention and your choices

We retain enquiry information for enquiry handling, related support and applicable record-keeping needs. You can ask about the retention period applying to your record, request access or correction, request deletion, or withdraw permission for further course follow-up by emailing info@techleadsit.com. Include the email or mobile number used for your enquiry; do not send passwords or verification codes. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations or dispute handling.

Security and payments

This form uses SMS verification and does not ask for payment-card details. Do not share your verification code with anyone. Our company Privacy Policy describes its data-security practices and explains that online systems cannot guarantee absolute security.

Cookies, external services and changes

Read our Cookie Policy for this page’s browser storage and attribution practices. External websites have their own notices. We will update this notice when the described practices change; the revision date appears above.
