Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains cookies and similar browser storage used on the AI Powered+ Digital Marketing landing page operated by Tech Leads IT Solutions Private Limited. Read it alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. What cookies and browser storage do

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. Session storage is a separate browser feature used to retain information during a browsing session. These technologies can support website functionality, link a course enquiry to a campaign and measure website use.

2. Course-enquiry session storage

The landing page uses the session-storage key tlit_dm_attribution_v1 to remember a generated session identifier, campaign parameters, landing-page address and referring-page address. When enabled, approximate city and state may also be saved. This record helps attach attribution information to your enquiry. The attribution record does not store your name, phone number, email address or OTP. Session storage normally lasts for the browser tab’s session; browser restore features may preserve it.

3. Analytics and advertising identifiers

The form can read existing _ga, _fbp and _fbc cookies, when present, and attach their identifiers to the CRM enquiry. These identifiers relate to Google Analytics and Meta measurement. The page also captures campaign parameters such as UTM values, GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID and FBCLID from the visiting URL. A URL parameter is not itself a cookie.

Google Tag Manager may load analytics or advertising tags configured by Tech Leads IT. Microsoft Clarity may be enabled separately or through a tag manager. Which cookies are set, and how long they remain, depends on the enabled tags, provider settings and browser restrictions. Not every visitor will receive every cookie. The form does not invent an analytics identifier when a provider cookie is absent.

4. How this relates to your enquiry

When the live form is enabled, verified contact information and available campaign attribution are sent to the course team’s TeleCRM system so the team can respond and understand the source of the enquiry. SMS verification uses the configured messaging provider. If enabled, an IP-based location service provides approximate city and state when the enquiry form opens; this does not use GPS permission. See the Privacy Policy for the wider use of personal information.

5. Managing cookies

You can inspect, block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser’s privacy settings. Browser controls for cookies and session storage may be separate. Clearing storage can remove campaign information or preferences. Blocking third-party services may affect measurement or some website functions.

If cookie-consent controls are presented, use them to choose the optional categories you allow. This policy page itself is an explanation, not a consent control. Contact us if you need help with your preferences. Promotional-email choices in the enquiry form are separate from cookie preferences.

6. Third-party information

For provider information, see Google’s cookie information, Meta’s cookie policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement. Third-party services control their own technologies and may change their practices.

7. Updates and contact

We may update this policy when website technologies or practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
