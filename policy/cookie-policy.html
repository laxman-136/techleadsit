Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains how Tech Leads IT Solutions Private Limited uses cookies and similar technologies on its Oracle Fusion SCM and AI Powered+ Digital Marketing landing pages. Read it alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. What cookies and browser storage do

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by your browser. Session storage is a separate browser feature used to retain information during a browsing session. These technologies can support website functionality, link a course enquiry to a campaign and measure website use.

2. Oracle Fusion SCM landing page

The SCM page uses the site’s shared Google Tag Manager container when configured. The container can load Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to measure visits and ad performance. When those tags run, they may use cookies such as _ga, _fbp or _fbc and receive information such as the page address, referrer, device information and campaign identifiers. Cookie use and duration depend on which tags run, their provider settings, your choices and your browser.

The SCM form keeps your answers in the open page while you move between steps. On final submission, the form sends the details you provided to Conversion Bridge, our WordPress lead inbox. The form sends the request type and registration identifier with your answers. Analytics and advertising tags are configured separately in the tag manager. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle SCM enquiries.

3. AI Powered+ Digital Marketing landing pages

Course-enquiry session storage

These pages may use the session-storage key tlit_dm_attribution_v1 to remember a generated session identifier, campaign parameters, landing-page address and referring-page address. When enabled, approximate city and state may also be saved. This record helps attach attribution information to your enquiry and contains campaign and visit details. Session storage normally lasts for the browser tab’s session, and browser restore features may preserve it.

Analytics and advertising identifiers

The Digital Marketing forms can read existing _ga, _fbp and _fbc cookies, when present, and attach their identifiers to the enquiry. These pages can capture campaign parameters such as UTM values, GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID and FBCLID from the visiting URL. Google Tag Manager may load configured analytics or advertising tags. Microsoft Clarity may be enabled separately or through a tag manager.

How this relates to your enquiry

Depending on the form configuration, contact details and available campaign attribution may be stored in the course team’s lead-management system or passed to TeleCRM for follow-up. Where enabled, SMS verification uses the configured messaging provider and IP-based location can provide approximate city and state. See the Privacy Policy for how we use enquiry information.

4. Managing cookies

You can inspect, block or delete cookies and site storage in your browser’s privacy settings. Browser controls for cookies and session storage may be separate. Clearing storage can remove campaign information or preferences. Blocking third-party services may affect measurement or some website functions.

Use the site’s cookie controls, where available, to set or change your preferences for optional technologies. Contact us if you need help with your preferences. Cookie preferences and promotional-email choices are managed separately.

5. Third-party information

For provider information, see Google’s cookie information, how Google uses data from partner sites, Meta’s cookie policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement. Third-party services control their own technologies and may change their practices.
