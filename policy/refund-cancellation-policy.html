Tech Leads IT Solutions Private Limited ("Tech Leads IT", "we", "our" or "us") wants learners to make an informed decision before enrolling. We provide course information and demo opportunities where available and offer the following seven-day refund period for eligible course enrolments purchased directly from Tech Leads IT.

1. Seven-day refund period

You may request cancellation and a refund within 7 calendar days from your enrolment date.

For this policy, the enrolment date is the date shown on the payment receipt, invoice or written enrolment confirmation issued by Tech Leads IT, whichever occurs first after payment is received.

A “7-day” period means seven consecutive calendar days, including weekends and public holidays. If the seventh day falls on a day when our support team is unavailable, an email received before 11:59 p.m. India Standard Time on that day will still be treated as submitted within the refund period.

2. How to request a refund

Send an email to info@techleadsit.com with the subject line:

Refund Request – [Learner Name] – [Course Name]

Include:

  • Learner’s full name.
  • Email address and phone number used for enrolment.
  • Course name and batch, if known.
  • Enrolment/payment date.
  • Invoice, receipt or transaction reference.
  • Reason for cancellation. The reason helps us improve and does not remove an otherwise valid right under this policy.

The email timestamp will be used to determine whether the request was submitted within seven calendar days. Requests made only by telephone, social media or verbal discussion should also be confirmed by email so there is a clear record.

3. Eligibility

A refund request is eligible under this policy when:

  • The course was purchased directly from Tech Leads IT.
  • The request is emailed within seven calendar days of the enrolment date.
  • The requester is the learner or the person who made the payment and can provide reasonable payment verification.
  • The payment has not already been reversed, charged back or refunded.

Access to classes, recordings, learning materials and practice environments will end when a cancellation is approved.

4. Requests after seven days

Course fees are generally non-refundable after the seven-calendar-day period has expired because training, platform access, recordings, trainer allocation and practice resources may already have been provided or reserved.

If exceptional circumstances prevent participation after the refund period, contact us. At our discretion, and without creating an automatic entitlement, we may offer a batch transfer, temporary deferral or another reasonable learning arrangement. Any mandatory rights available under applicable consumer law remain unaffected.

5. Items and charges not covered

Unless required by law or caused by an error by Tech Leads IT, the following are not included in a course-fee refund:

  • Third-party certification or examination fees paid directly to Oracle or another provider.
  • Bank, foreign-exchange or payment-provider charges that were not received by Tech Leads IT.
  • Separately purchased third-party licences, books or services that were clearly disclosed as non-refundable before purchase and have already been issued.
  • Services already completed in full before the refund request.

A promotional or discounted course remains eligible for the same seven-day refund period unless separate written terms were clearly disclosed and accepted before payment. No promotional term will limit rights that cannot legally be limited.

6. Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you are charged more than once for the same enrolment or an incorrect amount is collected, notify us promptly with the transaction details. After verification, the duplicate or excess amount will be refunded to the original payment method. This is separate from the seven-day course-cancellation period.

7. Cancellation or material change by Tech Leads IT

If Tech Leads IT cancels a paid course and cannot provide a reasonable alternative, you may choose:

  • A transfer to another suitable batch or course, subject to your agreement; or
  • A refund of the amount paid for the cancelled service.

If we make a material change to a confirmed course and the offered alternative is not reasonably suitable, contact us so that we can review an appropriate remedy.

8. Review and processing time

  • We will acknowledge or review a complete refund request within 5 business days.
  • We may contact you to verify the payment or clarify the request.
  • Once approved, we will initiate the refund within 10 business days.
  • Refunds will normally be sent to the original payment method.
  • Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to credit the amount after we initiate it.

If the original payment method cannot receive the refund, we may request verified bank details belonging to the payer. Do not send card passwords, PINs, CVVs or online-banking credentials.

9. Chargebacks and dispute resolution

Please contact us before initiating a bank chargeback so that we have an opportunity to investigate and resolve the request. Starting a chargeback does not increase or reduce rights available under applicable law, but parallel refund and chargeback processes can delay reconciliation.
