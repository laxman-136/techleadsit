Tech Leads IT Solutions Private Limited ("Tech Leads IT", "we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we use and share it, and the choices available to you when you visit our websites, submit an enquiry, register for a demo, enrol in a course, access our learning platform or communicate with us.

This policy applies to the Tech Leads IT landing pages, websites, course-enquiry forms, learning platforms and related services that link to it.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, city and country.
  • Your course or module of interest.
  • Your education, employment background, experience level and learning objectives when you choose to provide them.
  • Your preferred time for a call, demo or training batch.
  • Messages, enquiries and support communications sent by form, telephone, email or WhatsApp.
  • Enrolment information, billing details, payment reference and transaction status.
  • Account information required to provide access to classes, recordings, learning materials or practice environments.
  • Feedback, survey responses and testimonials submitted voluntarily.

We do not intentionally collect card numbers, CVVs or online-banking credentials on our servers. Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers under their own privacy and security practices.

Information collected automatically

When you use our websites or landing pages, we may collect:

  • IP address, browser type, device type and operating system.
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, session duration, referring page and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Cookie identifiers, consent status and website-performance information.
  • Advertising and attribution information such as UTM parameters, campaign identifiers, GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID and event identifiers.
  • Form-success, demo-request and course-enquiry events used to understand whether our website and advertising are working correctly.

We do not intentionally send sensitive personal information, form-field contents, phone numbers or email addresses to analytics platforms unless the relevant platform supports an approved, consented and protected method such as enhanced conversions.

2. How we use your information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide requested course, curriculum, fee, trainer, demo and batch information.
  • Contact you by telephone, WhatsApp, SMS or email about the enquiry you submitted.
  • Schedule demos, counselling calls, classes and learner-support sessions.
  • Process enrolment and payments and issue invoices or receipts.
  • Provide and manage learning-platform, course-recording and practice-instance access.
  • Deliver classes, learning materials, technical assistance, interview preparation and other services included in your enrolment.
  • Maintain customer-service and learner-support records.
  • Measure website performance, prevent duplicate enquiries and improve forms and landing pages.
  • Attribute enquiries to marketing campaigns and measure advertising performance.
  • Detect fraud, spam, security incidents and misuse.
  • Comply with accounting, tax, regulatory and legal obligations.
  • Send promotional communications where you have consented or where otherwise permitted by applicable law. You can opt out at any time.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, measure performance, understand visitor journeys, secure forms and evaluate advertising campaigns.

These technologies may be provided by Tech Leads IT or by service providers such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Google Tag Manager, Meta and other analytics, advertising or website-service platforms. Where consent is legally required, non-essential analytics or advertising technologies should be used only after consent is recorded.

You can control cookies through the consent controls made available on the website and through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect website functionality or attribution accuracy.

4. How we share information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary with:

  • CRM and lead-management providers, including systems used to route and respond to enquiries.
  • Website hosting, cloud, security and technical-support providers.
  • Learning-management, communication, webinar and practice-environment providers.
  • Payment gateways, banks and accounting providers.
  • Analytics and advertising platforms used to measure campaigns and improve marketing.
  • WhatsApp, telecommunications and email-service providers used to respond to your enquiry.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, regulators, law-enforcement agencies or courts where disclosure is required or legally justified.
  • A successor organisation in connection with a lawful merger, acquisition, restructuring or transfer of the business, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

5. Advertising measurement and server-side tracking

We use browser-side and server-side measurement to understand whether advertising leads to genuine course enquiries. A single enquiry may generate related technical events across our website, analytics systems, advertising platforms and CRM. We use event identifiers and other controls to reduce duplicate measurement.

Advertising platforms apply their own attribution rules. An advertising conversion does not necessarily mean that additional personal details have been disclosed to the platform.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including enquiry follow-up, course delivery, learner support, dispute resolution, fraud prevention and compliance with legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Retention periods may differ by record type. When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymise or securely restrict it, subject to technical and legal limitations.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, misuse, alteration, loss or disclosure. These measures may include access controls, secure connections, restricted credentials, monitoring and service-provider reviews.

No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary sensitive information through enquiry forms or ordinary email.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law and verification of your identity, you may request to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Withdraw consent for future processing where processing depends on consent.
  • Opt out of promotional calls, messages or emails.
  • Request deletion of information that is no longer required, subject to legal and contractual retention obligations.
  • Raise a complaint or grievance about our handling of your information.

To make a request, email info@techleadsit.com. We may ask for information necessary to verify your identity and locate your records.

Withdrawing marketing consent does not prevent us from sending service communications relating to an active enquiry, payment, enrolment, scheduled class or support request.

9. Children’s privacy

Our services are intended primarily for adults and career learners. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission from a parent or legal guardian. If you believe a child has submitted information without appropriate permission, contact us so that we can review and delete it where required.

10. Third-party websites and services

Our websites may link to third-party services, including payment providers, WhatsApp, social networks, video platforms, app stores and Oracle resources. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Tech Leads IT is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that we do not control.

11. International processing

Some service providers may process information outside your state or country. Where applicable, we take reasonable steps to use service providers and safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and applicable legal requirements.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, technologies or legal obligations. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Material changes may also be communicated through the website or other appropriate channels.
