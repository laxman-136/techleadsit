SECTION 4 — WHAT IS ORACLE FUSION SCM
H2: What Is Oracle Fusion SCM?
Oracle Fusion SCM (Supply Chain Management) is Oracle's cloud-based suite for managing the full flow of goods, services, and information — from raw material procurement to final delivery to the customer.
It includes tools for:
Procurement — Managing suppliers, purchase orders, and sourcing
Inventory Management — Tracking stock across warehouses and locations
Order Management — Processing and fulfilling customer orders
Supply Chain Planning — Forecasting demand and planning production
Manufacturing — Managing production work orders and shop-floor operations
Costing — Calculating the actual cost of items and processes
Quality Management — Enforcing inspection and quality standards
Reporting and Analytics — Monitoring supply chain performance with dashboards
Companies across industries — manufacturing, retail, logistics, pharma, oil and gas — run their supply chain on Oracle Fusion. Oracle-certified professionals who understand these modules are consistently in demand.

SECTION 6 — MODULES COVERED
H2: What You Will Learn — Full Module Breakdown
This is a complete Oracle Fusion SCM training program. You will learn every major module, starting from foundational concepts and progressing to real-world business scenarios.

📦 Module 1 — Procurement Supplier management, purchase requisitions, purchase orders, supplier negotiations, blanket agreements, approved supplier lists, invoice matching, and procurement business flows.
📦 Module 2 — Inventory Management Item setup, sub-inventory and locators, material transactions, inventory adjustments, cycle counting, physical inventory, FIFO and LIFO costing basics, and inter-org transfers.
📦 Module 3 — Order Management Sales order creation, order types, holds, scheduling, shipping, invoicing, returns (RMA), and end-to-end order-to-cash flow.
📦 Module 4 — Supply Chain Planning Demand management, supply planning, collaborative planning, constrained planning, plan runs, and exception management.
📦 Module 5 — Manufacturing Basics Work order creation, discrete manufacturing overview, shop-floor operations, and production completion flows.
📦 Module 6 — Quality Management Inspection plans, quality results, quality actions, and quality integration with inventory and procurement.
📦 Module 7 — Costing Cost organisation setup, standard costing, actual costing, cost elements, and cost accounting basics.
📦 Module 8 — Reporting Basics OTBI (Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence), subject areas for SCM, creating and running reports, and reading supply chain dashboards.
