What is Oracle Fusion SCM?

Oracle Fusion SCM (Supply Chain Management) is Oracle's next-generation, cloud-based enterprise suite that manages the entire lifecycle of goods, services, and information — from raw material sourcing to final delivery.

Instead of disconnected legacy software, Fusion SCM integrates all modules into a single, unified database in the cloud, giving businesses real-time visibility into their global supply chains.

Core Pillars Governed:

  • Procurement — Sourcing, supplier contracts, and PO processing.
  • Inventory Management — Multi-org stock levels, adjustments, and counting.
  • Order Management — Sales order entry, scheduling, shipping, and RMA.
  • Supply Chain Planning — Demand forecasting, supply planning, and plan runs.

Who Uses Oracle SCM?

Global corporations in manufacturing, retail, pharmaceuticals, oil & gas, logistics, and the public sector run their entire supply chains on Oracle Cloud.

Because these systems are highly critical, MNCs consistently pay premium salaries to functional experts who know how to set up, configure, and maintain them.
