What Does an Oracle Fusion SCM Consultant Do?

An Oracle Fusion SCM consultant helps companies set up and improve the software their teams use to purchase goods, manage inventory and fulfil customer orders.

Your role: business + Oracle

You connect what business teams need with how Oracle Fusion Cloud is configured and used.

  1. 1

    Understand the business

    Talk to purchasing, warehouse and sales teams to understand their processes and requirements.

  2. 2

    Configure Oracle

    Set up purchasing rules, approvals, inventory organisations, pricing and order processing.

  3. 3

    Prepare business data

    Help organise and upload items, suppliers, stock balances and other information the system needs.

  4. 4

    Test complete processes

    Check that purchase requests, approvals, receipts, inventory movements and customer orders work correctly together.

  5. 5

    Guide users and resolve issues

    Explain how to use the system, investigate problems and help teams adopt process changes.

A practical example

A purchase request gets stuck. You investigate the approval setup, help correct the issue and test the process with the purchasing team.
