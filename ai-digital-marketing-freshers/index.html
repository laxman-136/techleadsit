A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING.

Good questions. Clear answers.

Do I need previous marketing experience? +

No. The learning path begins with customers, business goals and digital marketing fundamentals. Bring a laptop and time to practise.

Do I need to know coding? +

The course uses WordPress, Elementor and no-code tools. You'll still learn technical concepts such as tracking and webhooks step by step.

Can I take the course while studying? +

Yes, if you can attend Monday–Friday, 8–9 PM IST and make time for project practice. Recordings are available for 6 months.

What is the course fee? +

The source programme lists an AI Powered+ offer fee of ₹44,999. Confirm the current fee, taxes, payment options and included tool access with the team before enrolling.

Will I get a guaranteed job or salary? +

The course provides practical learning, portfolio development and career preparation. Interview selection, job offers and salaries depend on your skills and the employer's requirements.

What will happen after I request a demo? +

For the live course, the team will contact you about the next available session. This design preview only demonstrates the form and OTP screens; it sends no enquiry.

Which tools are included, and for how long? +

The AI Powered+ course lists WordPress, Elementor Pro, Semrush, n8n Pro and private research tools. Ask about licence type, shared versus individual access, access duration and extra costs such as ad budgets, hosting or domains.

What are the payment and refund terms? +

The listed programme fee is ₹44,999. Ask the team for written confirmation of taxes, instalment options, cancellation and refund terms before paying.

What does internship and placement support include? +

The programme lists a 3-month supervised internship, portfolio guidance, resume preparation and mock interviews. Confirm the internship schedule, eligibility and support duration. Job offers and salaries are not guaranteed.

What will I see in the free demo? +

Explore the three projects, discuss how the course starts from the fundamentals, and ask about teaching, fees and the timetable. The team will confirm the demo’s date, duration and joining details.
