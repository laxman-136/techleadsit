Make sure our email reaches you.

When you receive an email from Tech Leads IT, keep it easy to find so you don’t miss your Live Session details.

Spam / JunkUpdatesPromotions
  1. Check your inbox first. If you don’t see our email, search for “Tech Leads IT” and check Spam, Updates and Promotions.
  2. Move it to Primary. In Gmail, move the email from Updates or Promotions to the Primary tab. If Gmail asks about future messages from this sender, choose Yes.
  3. If it’s in Spam, mark it “Not spam.” Then move it to Primary, or your main inbox if you use another email app. You can also add the sender to your contacts.
